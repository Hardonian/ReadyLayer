## Core narrative

AI coding agents increase change volume and can cross delivery boundaries faster than review processes adapt. ReadyLayer
focuses on three concrete jobs:

1. Classify risky changes and protected paths before merge.
2. Apply versioned, deterministic policy and record the decision inputs.
3. Export evidence and signed exceptions for review and audit workflows.

## Product pillars

### Deterministic policy

The local runner consumes schema-defined input and produces a repeatable output for the same input and policy version.
Link proof to `tools/ready-layer-runner/`.

### Portable evidence

Evidence bundles, attestations, and waiver signatures are inspectable outside the dashboard. Clearly label beta formats and
require evaluation in the buyer's environment.

### Controlled adoption

Begin with deterministic fixtures, move to shadow mode on one repository, then enforce only the accepted controls. Never
describe a pilot as generally available production service.

## Proof section

Use only repository-backed proof:

- Runner input/output schemas
- Demo contract tests
- Webhook verification tests
- Signed waiver and attestation tests
- Health and readiness contracts
- Deployment, rollback, and incident runbooks

Do not use anonymous testimonials, invented people or companies, adoption counters, unsupported performance numbers,
certification badges, or service-level claims.

## FAQ

### Is ReadyLayer a compliance certification?

No. ReadyLayer can produce technical governance evidence. Your organization, auditor, and counsel determine whether a
control satisfies a specific obligation.

### Does ReadyLayer replace CI, code review, or security tooling?

No. It composes with those systems and adds policy and evidence around agent-driven changes.

### Is managed hosting generally available?

Managed deployments are scoped through design-partner pilots. Topology, support, data handling, and service commitments
must be written into the applicable agreement.

### What should a buyer try first?

Inspect the audit example, verify a policy, run the credential-free sandbox, and review the trust center before connecting a
production repository.

## Measurement

Track funnel events without presenting internal metrics as customer outcomes:

- `evaluation_started`
- `audit_example_opened`
- `policy_verification_opened`
- `sandbox_started`
- `trust_center_opened`
- `pilot_request_submitted`

Activation is a completed sandbox run plus one inspected evidence artifact. A qualified pilot request identifies a
repository, risk boundary, champion, and accountable approver.
